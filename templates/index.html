    لحياة مالية أذكى

    YARIN يارين is a public multi-user personal finance platform designed to help people plan, track and understand their financial life from one secure place. It brings together income, expenses, savings, shopping planning, family transfers, EMI, debt, notes, reports, calculators, exchange-rate tools and Azret AI assistance while keeping each account's records separated.

    ✓ Responsive on phone, tablet and desktop✓ Local device time automatically follows the user's country✓ AED / INR currency tools and reports✓ Secure password-reset flow with email OTP✓ Backup, export and personal finance insights

    Settings

    PERSONALIZATION & PAY SCHEDULE

    Account Preferences

    Manage how YARIN looks, which currencies it uses, and when your monthly salary is credited.

    Theme

    Choose the appearance you prefer across YARIN.

    Currency Pair

    Choose the two currencies used across your account. The top switch, amount hints, calculator and exchange chart update automatically.

    Salary Credit Schedule

    Set the day you normally receive your salary. The Dashboard countdown and salary reminder will use this date automatically.

    For shorter months, YARIN automatically uses the last valid day of that month.
